Demo Showcase Team Poster
In advance of the Demo Showcase, every team must design a 13" x 19" foam-core poster with an easel back that will be displayed at the team tables. You must use the provided template linked below and include your finalized team name on the slide. Do not include your team number on the poster. Once submitted here, the program administrators will have the posters printed and will bring them to the event before check-in begins (do NOT print your own poster). Teams must display a poster on their table. 

Teams may design within the white space on the poster, but may not remove, change, cover, or manipulate the branded header of the template. 

Suggestions on what to include: logo, one line pitch, any images or graphs, etc. Just like a good presentation slide, the poster should not be too crowded with words. It should be primarily visual to draw someone to their table who might be walking by, and should help represent your venture.  Remember, people need to be able to see and understand it from about 6-8 feet away and you want the poster to support your pitch, not distract from it. The time that judges may spend reading words on your poster takes time away from them listening to your pitch and asking questions.
